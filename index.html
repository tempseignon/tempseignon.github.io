Overview

This draft dashboard counts civil service appointments and resignations announced each year by NYC agencies, using live data from the City Record Online on NYC Open Data. It is a first step toward looking at how often people move into and out of civil service roles between exam cycles.

Note: these are counts of personnel notices per year, not turnover rates, and the current year is incomplete. Employee names and other personal details are never loaded onto this page.
